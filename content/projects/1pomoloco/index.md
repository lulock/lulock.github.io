Pomoloco :tomato: is a pomodoro technique Terminal User Interface (TUI) app written in Go with the help of [BubbleTea](https://github.com/charmbracelet/bubbletea):bubble_tea:.

It features random motivational quotes for each session if you have a working internet connection (fetched from [zenquotes.io](https://zenquotes.io/)).

You can also configure your preferred colour theme, pomo timer duration, and loco (break) duration.

In future, there will be the option to document progress of your pomodori with guided reflections.
