I'm a PhD student in computer science and member of the [Responsible Artificial Intelligence group](https://www.umu.se/en/research/groups/responsible-artificial-intelligence/) at Umeå University. My research is in Explainable Artificial Intelligence where I focus not only on the technical aspects of explanation generation, but also on how social interactions shape the impact of those methods in practice.

Currently, I am working on building a modular, end-to-end explainability pipeline to aid in my research. For fun, I work on mini projects both in Python and, more recently, Go. You can check out my research output [here]({{< ref "/research" >}}) and my hobby projects [here]({{< ref "/projects" >}}).
