I'm a software engineer with a background in artificial intelligence research.

I received my PhD in computer science from Umeå University. My research in explainable artificial intelligence focused not only on the technical aspects of explanation generation, but also on how social interactions shape the impact of those methods in practice. During my time at the computing science department, I was also a member of the [Responsible Artificial Intelligence group](https://www.umu.se/en/research/groups/responsible-artificial-intelligence/).

For fun, I work on mini projects in Python and, more recently, Go, TypeScript, and Java. You can check out my research output [here]({{< ref "/research" >}}) and my hobby projects [here]({{< ref "/projects" >}}).
